Sources & Contributing

Where the numbers come from, and how to fix them.

Sources

  • FFCC World Ultimania (Square Enix, Japanese). Monster stats, boss data, dungeon-level scaling, the artifact chart and the tribe frame data. Where sources disagree, this one wins.
  • Nintendo Power official guide (GameCube). Boss reward sets and bonus point thresholds.
  • Piggyback official guide (French). Damage formulas.
  • GameFAQs FAQs and community spreadsheets. Items, recipes, chests, enemy drops and walkthrough notes.
  • The FFCC speedrun community, including Linitaa's translations of the Ultimania tribe and artifact charts.
  • Game data research from FFCC modding and randomizer projects.

All Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles names, images and sprites are © Square Enix. This is a non-commercial fan project.

Fixing or adding data

Every page reads its numbers from the files in the data/ folder, so you never need to touch the page code to correct a number:

  1. Open the matching file, e.g. data/enemies.js for a monster.
  2. Edit the value. If you're unsure of something, use null rather than guessing.
  3. Mention your source in the entry's source or notes field.

data/README.md explains every field.
